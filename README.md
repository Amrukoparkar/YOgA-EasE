
# 🧘‍♀️ Yoga Ease  

**Yoga Ease** is a web-based platform designed to help individuals explore yoga, join expert-led sessions, and access valuable resources for a balanced lifestyle. It promotes health, mindfulness, and overall well-being through diverse yoga practices and community support.  

🌐 Live Demo: [Yoga Ease](https://amrukoparkar.github.io/YOgA-EasE/)  

---

## ✨ Features  
- 🧘 **Explore Yoga Styles** – Learn different yoga forms suitable for beginners to advanced levels.  
- 🎥 **Expert-Led Sessions** – Guided practices for better posture, flexibility, and mindfulness.  
- 📚 **Resources & Tips** – Access articles, guides, and yoga benefits.  
- 👥 **Community Support** – Connect with like-minded individuals on their wellness journey.  
- 🌿 **Simple & User-Friendly Interface** – Easy navigation and responsive design.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- **HTML5** – Structure & Content  
- **CSS3** – Styling & Design  
- **JavaScript (Vanilla JS)** – Interactivity & Functionality  

---

## 🚀 How to Use  
1. Visit the live site: [Yoga Ease](https://amrukoparkar.github.io/YOgA-EasE/)  
2. Explore different yoga styles and sessions.  
3. Sign up for resources and community support.  
4. Begin your journey toward a healthier and balanced life.
